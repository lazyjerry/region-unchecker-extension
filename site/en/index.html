27 EU countries.
Unchecked in one go.

Until you have declared your trader status under the EU Digital Services Act (DSA), your app's availability must exclude the EU. This Chrome extension unchecks those countries on your publishing console's country/region list for you.

Install the extension See how it works

Why you need it

Google Play Console has no "exclude EU" option, and its country availability API is read-only. Every release or region change means searching for a country, unchecking it, then searching for the next one. That's 27 countries plus the French overseas regions, and one slip can leave your app available where it shouldn't be.

How it works

  1. Open the country/region editorGo to your app's availability editor in Google Play Console or App Store Connect.
  2. Choose the countriesClick the extension icon, pick a list such as "EU member states (27)", or add other names.
  3. Preview, then uncheckPreview outlines the countries in red. When it looks right, click Uncheck.
  4. Review and save yourselfThe extension never clicks Save, so you get a last look before submitting.

Features

  • Three built-in listsEU member states (27), French overseas regions (when listed separately) and Mainland China. Mix as you need.
  • Extra namesOne country name per line. Only exact matches count, so "Norway" or a "Select all Europe" box is never touched.
  • Preview modeHighlights without clicking, so you can check first.
  • Uses the search boxCountries not visible on the page are looked up one by one through the page's search box.
  • Clear resultsLists what was unchecked, what was already unchecked and what was not found. Nothing is skipped silently.
  • English and Traditional ChineseCountry names cover English plus Taiwan and Hong Kong Chinese spellings.

Privacy

No network access and no data collection. Your choices stay in your browser, and the extension runs on the current tab only when you click a button. See the privacy policy.

Manual install (developer mode)
  1. Download the extension zip and unzip it.
  2. Open chrome://extensions in Chrome and turn on Developer mode at the top right.
  3. Click "Load unpacked" and select the unzipped folder.

FAQ

Does it click Save for me?

No. It only unchecks boxes. Saving or submitting is always up to you.

Some countries show as "Not found". What now?

The console probably uses a different name. Paste the name exactly as shown into "Other names", one per line, and run it again.

Does it work on App Store Connect?

Yes. App Store Connect also offers an API for availability, so if you already automate it, the API is more robust.

Will Iceland, Norway, Switzerland or the UK be unchecked?

No. They are not EU members and are not in the default lists. Matching is exact, so similarly named countries are never clicked.

Who decides whether to exclude the EU?

You do. This is an operating tool, not legal advice. Whether you need to declare DSA trader status depends on your situation.
