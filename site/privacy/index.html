隱私權政策

最後更新：2026-11-10

「上架地區批次取消」Chrome 擴充套件不收集、不傳送、不出售任何個人資料。

  • 套件不連網，沒有任何伺服器。
  • 你在套件中選擇的國家群組與補充名稱，只存在瀏覽器本機（chrome.storage.local），移除套件即刪除。
  • 套件只在你按下「預覽」或「取消勾選」時，在目前分頁讀取國家清單並點擊對應的勾選框，不讀取或保存頁面上的其他內容。

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-11-10

The "Region Bulk Unchecker" Chrome extension does not collect, transmit or sell any personal data.

  • The extension makes no network requests and has no server.
  • The country groups and extra names you choose are stored only in your browser (chrome.storage.local) and are removed when you uninstall the extension.
  • The extension reads the country list and clicks the matching checkboxes on the current tab only when you click Preview or Uncheck. It does not read or keep any other page content.
